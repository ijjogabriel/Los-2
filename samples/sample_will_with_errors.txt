LAST WILL AND TESTAMENT OF JOHN ROBERT WILLIAMS

I, John Robert Williams, a resident of Hennepin County, Minnesota, being of sound and
disposing mind and memory and not acting under duress, menace, fraud, or undue
influence of any person, do hereby make, publish, and declare this to be my Last Will
and Testament.


ARTICLE I — REVOCATION OF PRIOR WILLS

I hereby revoke any and all prior wills, codicils, and testamentary dispositions
previously made by me, and declare this to be my sole and complete Last Will and
Testament.


ARTICLE II — FAMILY

I am currently married to Margaret Williams. I have three children: David Williams
and Sara Williams, who are my children from a prior marriage, and Michael Williams,
age fourteen (14), who is my child from my current marriage. All references in this
Will to "my children" include all three of the above-named individuals.


ARTICLE III — SPECIFIC BEQUESTS

I give, bequeath, and devise the following specific gifts from my estate:

  (a) Real Property. I give my family home located at 841 Elm Street, Hennepin
      County, Minnesota, together with all appurtenances thereto, to my spouse,
      Margaret Williams, if she survives me by thirty (30) days. If Margaret Williams
      does not survive me by thirty (30) days, this bequest shall lapse and pass
      as part of my residuary estate.

  (b) Brokerage Account. I give my Schwab brokerage account, valued at approximately
      Three Hundred Forty Thousand Dollars ($340,000) at the time of this Will's
      execution, in equal shares to my children David Williams and Sara Williams, or
      to the survivor of them. If neither David Williams nor Sara Williams survives
      me, this bequest shall pass as part of my residuary estate.

  (c) Vehicle. I give my 2019 Toyota Camry, or any vehicle I may own at the time of
      my death that is substantially similar, to my son Michael Williams, if he
      survives me.


ARTICLE IV — RESIDUARY ESTATE

I give the rest, residue, and remainder of my estate, both real and personal, of
whatever kind and wherever situated, to my spouse, Margaret Williams, if she
survives me. If Margaret Williams does not survive me, I give the residuary estate
in equal shares to my children, David Williams, Sara Williams, and Michael Williams,
or to the survivors of them.


ARTICLE V — PERSONAL REPRESENTATIVE

I appoint my spouse, Margaret Williams, as Personal Representative of this Will and
my estate. My Personal Representative shall serve without bond. If Margaret Williams
is unable or unwilling to serve or to continue to serve as Personal Representative,
I appoint Elizabeth Williams as successor Personal Representative, also to serve
without bond.

My Personal Representative is authorized to: (i) sell, mortgage, or lease any
property of the estate; (ii) invest and reinvest estate assets; (iii) distribute
estate assets in cash or in kind; and (iv) exercise all other powers conferred by
the laws of Minnesota.


ARTICLE VI — TRUST FOR MINOR CHILD

I direct that any property passing to Michael Williams under this Will shall be held
in trust by Margaret Williams as Trustee until Michael Williams attains the age of
twenty-five (25) years, at which time the trust shall terminate and the entire
principal and accumulated income shall be distributed to Michael Williams outright
and free of trust.

Until the trust terminates, the Trustee may use so much of the net income and
principal of the trust as the Trustee, in the Trustee's sole discretion, deems
necessary for the health, education, maintenance, and support of Michael Williams.


ARTICLE VII — GUARDIANSHIP

In the event that Michael Williams is a minor at the time of my death, I nominate
and appoint Margaret Williams as Guardian of the person and property of Michael
Williams. If Margaret Williams is unable or unwilling to serve as Guardian, I
nominate and appoint David Williams as successor Guardian.


ARTICLE VIII — PET TRUST

I hereby establish a pet trust for the care and maintenance of my family dog, Max,
a Golden Retriever. I direct that the sum of Five Thousand Dollars ($5,000) be
set aside from my estate and held in trust for the benefit of Max. The Trustee
shall use the funds solely for veterinary care, food, shelter, and other reasonable
expenses for Max's well-being. Upon the death of Max, any remaining funds shall
revert to my residuary estate. The establishment of this pet trust is authorized
pursuant to Minn. Stat. § 524.3-407, which provides for the creation of trusts for
the care of designated animals.


ARTICLE IX — GENERAL PROVISIONS

(a) Survivorship. No person shall be deemed to have survived me unless such person
    is living on the thirtieth (30th) day after the date of my death.

(b) Governing Law. This Will shall be construed and administered in accordance
    with the laws of the State of Minnesota, which requires three (3) witnesses
    for a valid Last Will and Testament.

(c) Severability. If any provision of this Will is held to be invalid or
    unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.
